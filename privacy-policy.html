SDDA Elite randomizer Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

SDDA Elite randomizer generates random setups for the SDDA Elite class. The app generates and displays results on your device.

Data the app handles

The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information or usage data. It requires no account and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking. The developer does not receive or retain your randomizer results.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of an email you sent us, email chrojoh@gmail.com. If you contact the developer, your email address and message are used to respond and kept only as needed to handle your inquiry.
